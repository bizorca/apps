# The six formats

Every post ends with a `Go!` section and delivers something the reader can use
today. No theory, no motivation, no generalities.

**before_noon** — one action framed as today's assignment. Two or three
sentences of setup, the exact steps, then what they will have by end of day.
Title: `Before Noon: [specific action]`

**gut_check** — five to eight diagnostic questions applied to one area, right
now. No answers given; the questions do the work. Close with a threshold: "if
you said no to more than two of these, you have your project for the week."
Title: `Gut Check: [question]`

**steal_this** — one real tactic from a named real business or author,
reverse-engineered into components so it is replicable. Attribute it. Do not
editorialize about how brilliant it is.
Title: `Steal This: [tactic]`

**the_upgrade** — side by side: what most practices do, what to do instead. Both
sides concrete. Show the delta; do not moralize.
Title: `The Upgrade: [what to stop doing]`

**the_protocol** — a numbered operating procedure for one narrow task. Not
strategy — something you could hand an employee and have them run it today.
Title: `The Protocol: [task]`

**one_number** — open on a single metric or threshold. What it means, how to
measure it, and what to do depending on where they land.
Title: `[The number]: [what it means]`
