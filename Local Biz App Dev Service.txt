After launch, a $75 per month maintenance plan covers hosting, updates, and ongoing support. The business gets an app built to its exact operation, not a template stretched to fit.

The wrong approach is starting with the technology. No-code frameworks and AI coding tools are inputs, not the product. Start instead with three to five free or discounted builds for local businesses in a single category: restaurants, salons, or independent retailers. That friction keeps clients on the plan. Local business consultants who carry trust with the buyer are the distribution path. White-labelling the service at $10K to $25K per package opens a second revenue channel without adding acquisition cost.
